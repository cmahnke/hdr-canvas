HDRWebGPURenderer

HDRWebGPURenderer(parametersopt)

HDR enabled WebGPURenderer.

Note: Since three.js r180 a plain WebGPURenderer with outputType: HalfFloatType already delivers an HDR canvas (rgba16float with extended tone mapping). Only use this renderer when you need the rec2100-hlg canvas color space (BT.2020 primaries).
